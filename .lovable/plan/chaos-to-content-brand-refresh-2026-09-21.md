# Chaos To Content brand refresh

## Direction

Refresh the public-facing brand around the **Kinetic Creative Mind** concept: a lively, editorial visualization of a busy creator brain turning scattered thoughts into usable content. It should feel intelligent, energetic, and relatable to ADHD and executive-dysfunction experiences—never childish, medical, or tacky.

## Opening section

- Replace the microphone and waveform centerpiece with a custom abstract brain/mind system.
- Arrange screenshots, notes, links, unfinished captions, voice snippets, files, sparks, and post fragments around the brain in a controlled broken-grid composition.
- Show the visual transformation from scattered inputs toward organized, publishable content without implying voice is the primary input.
- Use subtle independent movement for idea fragments, connecting paths, and small sparks; keep the brain visually stable and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Replace “Brain Dump To Content Workspace” with **“Capture the chaos before it disappears.”**
- Keep **“Chaos in. Content out.”** as the dominant headline.
- Rewrite the supporting copy to speak directly to influencers, creators, social media managers, and offer-led business owners who lose ideas or freeze when it is time to post.
- Preserve **“Built for busy brains with more ideas than finished posts.”** as a prominent supporting line.
- Keep the primary signup action clear and visible.

## Visual system

- Apply the selected **Highlighter Air** palette:
  - soft pink `#FBE3F7`
  - pale cyan `#E5FBFF`
  - pastel electric yellow `#E9FF38`
  - near-black `#0A0A0A`
- Replace the current orange highlight token with pastel electric yellow across public pages and product screens so buttons and accents stay consistent.
- Use **Syne** for display headings and **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body text.
- Preserve the existing bold black outlines and neo-brutalist character, while refining spacing and hierarchy to feel more editorial and premium.
- Keep the overall site light; use the selected Kinetic Creative Mind prototype for composition, density, and motion—not its dark palette or gradients.

## Social proof

- Remove the “Don’t take our word for it” heading, invented testimonials, avatars, and quote cards for now.
- Remove the section cleanly rather than leaving a large empty band, allowing pricing to follow naturally from the preceding content.

## Supporting brand cleanup

- Replace any remaining prominent microphone-first or recording-first decorative branding on the public site with the broader scattered-ideas language.
- Keep voice notes listed as one supported input alongside text, screenshots, files, rants, and links.
- Update page metadata typography/loading where needed to support the new font pairing without changing product functionality.

## Validation

- Check the refreshed opening section at desktop and mobile sizes for readable text, balanced visual density, and no overlaps.
- Confirm the new yellow has replaced orange everywhere it is used as a brand highlight.
- Confirm the testimonial section is fully absent and the page flows directly into pricing.
- Verify animations remain subtle, the reduced-motion version is calm, and signup/navigation actions still work.

## Technical notes

- Build the busy-brain artwork as a responsive in-app composition using semantic color tokens, CSS, icons, and lightweight vector shapes rather than embedding the uploaded screenshot.
- Update global font configuration and semantic palette tokens, then adapt existing public components to those tokens.
- Keep backend behavior, dashboard workflows, and existing capture functionality unchanged.
